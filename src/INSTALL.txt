FINGER THIS — Scales, Tuner & Metronome

ANDROID (FingerThis.apk)
  1. Copy FingerThis.apk to your phone, or download it there
     (email it to yourself, Google Drive, USB cable…).
  2. Tap the file. Android asks to allow installs from that app
     (Files, Chrome, Gmail…): tap Settings, turn on "Allow from this source",
     go back and tap Install.
  3. Google Play Protect may say it doesn't recognize the developer.
     Tap "More details" > "Install anyway". (It's unrecognized because it
     isn't from the Play Store.)
  4. Open Finger This. It runs full-screen in landscape and works offline.
     The first time you use the Tuner or Rec, tap Allow for the microphone.
  Recordings you save go to Downloads/Finger This.
  To update later, install the new APK over the old one; your settings
  and practice log are kept.

WINDOWS
  Quick try: unzip, then double-click index.html (opens in Edge or Chrome).

  Install as an app (own window, Start-menu entry, works offline):
    1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop (free account).
    2. Drag the whole "finger-this" folder onto the page.
    3. Open the address Netlify gives you in Edge, then
       ⋯ menu > Apps > "Install this site as an app".

KEYBOARD (Windows)
  Space = start/stop    Up/Down = tempo ±1    Shift+Up/Down = ±5
